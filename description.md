# english

This is a small practice mod designed to make Inferno more fun to play. 

My goal is to make Inferno both more competative, and more *interesting*. In the base game too many of Inferno's units are only interesting after upgrade (gogs, hounds and pit fiends), and too many are just completely vanilla. At the same time, too many of their heros have actively janky specials (starting with spells they can't cast, which are also actively bad, for example). 

Changes currently include: 
* Familiars grant +1 damage to all damage spells. (that is, +1 damage EACH).
* Hellhounds and cerberi are now "hungry" - if their first attack does not kill anything, they will make a second attack.

* Various tweaks to spell list to make fire magic less bad:
- Dimension door is now Fire magic.
- Fireball to lvl 2, inferno to lvl 3, blind to lvl 4, slayer to lvl 3, misfortune to lvl 1.
- Fireball and misfortune cheaper casting cost.

* Fixing some of the heroes to make them less jank:
- All heretics start with 2 knowledge (so they can cast starting spells)
- All heretics start with fire magic rather than wisdom.
- Octavia and Rashkar (scholars) now start with spell books. They have view earth and curse (respectively)
- Zydar starts with magic arrow (so he can use sorcery)
- Ayden starts with curse
- Axsis starts with stone skin

* Some more experimental tweaks:
- Pyre starts with a cannon (and cannon specialty, similar to Jeremy)
- Olema is now a misfortune specialist. She casts Misfortune at expert level, and reduces luck by four.
- Fiona gives her hounds ADDITIONAL hunger attacks, +1 hunger for every three levels. (Hence, if she is level five, a hellhound would either attack until it killed something, or stop at 4.
- Inferno blacksmith now produces ballista. Dungeon blacksmith now produces ammo cart. (Inferno only has one shooter, and that shooter has plenty of shots naturally).



NOTE: For the time being, I have deliberately pushed TOO FAR - probably some of the hero specials I will need to wheel back in, or cancel. Potentially the power up on familiars goes too far (I suspect it doesn't... but it might).
The plan is to release this to people, and then reign in the changes which are either too weird or too powerful. (Most likely Fiona's special on weirdness grounds, and familiars special on power grounds).
